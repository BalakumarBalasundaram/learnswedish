# 14 – Inversion + description + passive
**Pattern:** Bisats + huvudsats + relativsats + passiv bisats

```
▷ BISATS ──▶ ■ HUVUDSATS ──▶ ◆ RELATIVSATS ──▶ ▣ PASSIV BISATS
```

🧠 **Remember it as "when…then…which was done":** time first, then who, then what was made of them.

> *När terminen var slut* **fick alla elever** *som hade jobbat hårt* **betyg** *som hade bestämts av lärarna*.
> *(When the term was over, all students who had worked hard got grades that had been decided by the teachers.)*
