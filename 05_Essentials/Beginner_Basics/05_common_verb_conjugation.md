# Common Verb Conjugation Drill

> 🧠 **Mental model — one row, one verb, all pronouns:** drill each verb horizontally across jag/du/han/vi/de first (present tense only), before mixing tenses. Horizontal repetition builds the "shape" of the verb faster than jumping between different verbs.

## 1. Present Tense — the 10 Most Useful Verbs

| Infinitiv | Presens | English |
|---|---|---|
| att vara | är | to be |
| att ha | har | to have |
| att göra | gör | to do/make |
| att vilja | vill | to want |
| att kunna | kan | to can/be able to |
| att få | får | to get/may |
| att gå | går | to go/walk |
| att komma | kommer | to come |
| att se | ser | to see |
| att veta | vet | to know |

> Swedish present tense does **not** change by pronoun — one form fits jag/du/han/hon/vi/ni/de. This is different from English and makes present tense the easiest to drill.

## 2. Same Verb, Every Pronoun (practice pattern)

| Pronoun | att vilja (vill) | English |
|---|---|---|
| jag | jag vill | I want |
| du | du vill | you want |
| han/hon/den/det | han vill | he wants |
| vi | vi vill | we want |
| ni | ni vill | you (pl.) want |
| de | de vill | they want |

## 3. Three Tenses at a Glance (regular verb example: att prata)

| Tense | Form | Example | English |
|---|---|---|---|
| Presens | pratar | Jag pratar svenska. | I speak Swedish. |
| Preteritum | pratade | Jag pratade svenska igår. | I spoke Swedish yesterday. |
| Perfekt | har pratat | Jag har pratat svenska förut. | I have spoken Swedish before. |

## 4. Drill Table — Fill in the Blanks (self-test)

| Svenska | English |
|---|---|
| Jag ___ (vara) trött. | I am tired. |
| Du ___ (ha) en bil. | You have a car. |
| Vi ___ (vilja) åka hem. | We want to go home. |
| De ___ (kunna) simma. | They can swim. |
| Han ___ (komma) imorgon. | He is coming tomorrow. |

## 5. Sample Sentences Combining Verbs

| Svenska | English |
|---|---|
| Jag vill kunna prata svenska flytande. | I want to be able to speak Swedish fluently. |
| Vi ska gå och se filmen ikväll. | We are going to go see the movie tonight. |
| Han vet inte var han ska gå. | He doesn't know where he should go. |
