# Colors & Common Adjectives

> 🧠 **Mental model — three endings, one rule:** most adjectives change by gender/number: **en**-word → base form, **ett**-word → **+t**, plural/bestämd → **+a**. Learn colors through this one rule instead of memorizing three separate words per color.

## 1. Colors (en-word / ett-word / plural)

| Svenska (en) | Svenska (ett) | Svenska (plural) | English |
|---|---|---|---|
| röd | rött | röda | red |
| blå | blått | blåa | blue |
| gul | gult | gula | yellow |
| grön | grönt | gröna | green |
| svart | svart | svarta | black |
| vit | vitt | vita | white |
| brun | brunt | bruna | brown |
| grå | grått | gråa | grey |
| orange | orange | orange | orange |
| rosa | rosa | rosa | pink |

> Example: en röd bil (a red car) · ett rött hus (a red house) · röda bilar (red cars)

## 2. Common Opposite Adjectives

| Svenska | English | Motsats (opposite) | English |
|---|---|---|---|
| stor | big | liten | small |
| ny | new | gammal | old |
| bra | good | dålig | bad |
| snabb | fast | långsam | slow |
| lätt | easy/light | svår / tung | hard / heavy |
| billig | cheap | dyr | expensive |
| ren | clean | smutsig | dirty |
| varm | warm | kall | cold |
| öppen | open | stängd | closed |

## 3. Neutrum & Plural Endings (quick rule)

| Rule | Example (en → ett → plural) |
|---|---|
| add **-t** for ett-words | stor → stor**t** |
| add **-a** for plural/bestämd form | stor → stor**a** |
| words already ending in vowel/consonant cluster often stay the same | bra → bra → bra |

## 4. Sample Sentences

| Svenska | English |
|---|---|
| Jag har en blå tröja. | I have a blue sweater. |
| Huset är gammalt men fint. | The house is old but nice. |
| Den här väskan är för dyr. | This bag is too expensive. |
| Bilarna är röda och svarta. | The cars are red and black. |
| Kaffet är varmt. | The coffee is warm. |

- Pick 5 objects around you and describe each with a color + one adjective.
