# Beginner Basics

> 🧠 **Mental model — build the skeleton first:** learn greetings → pronouns → numbers → question words in that order. These four groups are the "skeleton" every later sentence hangs on — don't move to grammar until you can say them without thinking.

## 1. Alphabet & Pronunciation (the extra 3 letters)

| Letter | Sounds like | Example word | English |
|---|---|---|---|
| å | "o" in "more" | båt | boat |
| ä | "e" in "bear" | äpple | apple |
| ö | "u" in "fur" | ö | island |

| Tricky sound | Rule of thumb | Example |
|---|---|---|
| sj-ljud | soft "sh/wh" blend | sju (seven), stjärna (star) |
| tj-ljud | soft "ch" (no "t") | tjej (girl), kött (meat) |
| k before e/i/y/ä/ö | "ch" sound | kök (kitchen), kyla (cold) |
| g before e/i/y/ä/ö | "y" sound | gata → **no** (hard g); ge (give) → soft |

## 6. First Sentences to Say Out Loud

| Svenska | English |
|---|---|
| Jag heter ... | My name is ... |
| Jag kommer från ... | I come from ... |
| Jag bor i ... | I live in ... |
| Jag pratar lite svenska. | I speak a little Swedish. |
| Kan du prata långsammare? | Can you speak more slowly? |
| Jag förstår inte. | I don't understand. |

## Practice
- Say the greetings table out loud, then cover the English column and reconstruct it.
- Count 0–20 forward and backward daily until automatic.
