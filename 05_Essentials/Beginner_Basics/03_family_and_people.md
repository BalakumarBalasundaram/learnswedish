# Family & People

> 🧠 **Mental model — draw your own family tree:** as you learn each word, point to (or picture) the real family member it maps to. Attaching a real face to each word makes it stick faster than a plain list.

## 1. Immediate Family

| Svenska | English |
|---|---|
| familj | family |
| mamma / mor | mom / mother |
| pappa / far | dad / father |
| syster | sister |
| bror | brother |
| dotter | daughter |
| son | son |
| barn | child/children |

## 2. Extended Family

| Svenska | English |
|---|---|

## 4. Describing People (basic adjectives)

|---|---|

## 5. Sample Sentences

| Svenska | English |
|---|---|
| Det här är min familj. | This is my family. |
| Min bror är äldre än jag. | My brother is older than me. |
| Jag har två systrar och en bror. | I have two sisters and one brother. |
| Min mormor är jättesnäll. | My grandmother is very kind. |
| Han är gift med min kusin. | He is married to my cousin. |
