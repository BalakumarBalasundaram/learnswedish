
#  Verb with Preposition Pattern:
    - **skriva + prep** = verb with preposition (often changes meaning significantly)
    - The preposition can indicate location, direction, purpose, or other relationships, and it often changes the meaning of the verb in a way that is not always predictable, so it must be learned as a set phrase.

## skriva med olika types av prepositioner:
- **skriva i**: to write in (a book, a notebook)
    - *Example*: 
        - Jag skriver i min dagbok varje kväll (*I write in my diary every night*). - infinitive -> refers to the act of writing in something
        - Jag skrev i min bok igår (*I wrote in my book yesterday*). - preteritum -> refers to the past action of writing in something
        - Jag har skrivit i min dagbok i flera år (*I have written in my diary for several years*). - perfekt -> refers to the completed action with relevance to the present -> also -> refers to the ongoing state of having written in the diary for several years
        - Jag hade skrivit i min dagbok innan jag gick ut (*I had written in my diary before I went out*). - pluskvamperfekt -> refers to the action completed before another past action
        - Jag ska skriva i min dagbok ikväll (*I will write in my diary tonight*). - futurum -> refers to a future action
        - Jag måste skriva i min dagbok varje dag (*I must write in my diary every day*). - modal verb + infinitive -> refers to the necessity of writing in the diary regularly
        - Jag kan skriva i min dagbok när jag vill (*I can write in my diary whenever I want*). - modal verb + infinitive -> refers to the ability to write in the diary at any time
