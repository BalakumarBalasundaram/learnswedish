# verbs used in automotive (fordon)
| Verb         | English Translation | Example Sentence in Swedish                          | English Translation of Example Sentence
|--------------|---------------------|----------------------------------------------------|--------------------------------------------------|
| köra          | to drive            | Jag kör min bil till jobbet varje dag.                 | I drive my car to work every day.
| reparera       | to repair           | Han reparerar sin bil i garaget.                 | He repairs his car in the garage.
| tvätta         | to wash             | Vi tvättar vår bil varje helg.                 | We wash our car every weekend.
| underhålla     | to maintain         | Det är viktigt att underhålla bilen regelbundet.                 | It is important to maintain the car regularly.

| parkera        | to park             | Hon parkerar sin bil på gatan.                | She parks her car on the street.
