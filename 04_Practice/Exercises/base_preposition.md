# 🚀 Base Verb + Prepositions

Below is a detailed explanation of each verb and its common prepositional combinations:

---

## SKRIVA (to write)
**Base Verb**: skriva — Refers to the act of writing.

**Prepositional Combinations**:
- **skriva upp**: To write down or note something for later use.  
  *Example*: Jag måste skriva upp det här numret (*I need to write down this number*).
- **skriva ut**: To print or write out.  
  *Example*: Kan du skriva ut dokumentet? (*Can you print the document?*).
- **skriva in**: To register or fill in.  
  *Example*: Du måste skriva in ditt namn här (*You need to fill in your name here*).
- **skriva ner**: To jot down or record.  
  *Example*: Jag skrev ner vad han sa (*I wrote down what he said*).

---

## SLUTA (to stop)
**Base Verb**: sluta — Refers to stopping or ending something.

**Prepositional Combinations**:
- **sluta upp**: To stop or cease.  
  *Example*: Sluta upp med det där! (*Stop that!*).
- **sluta med**: To quit or stop doing something.  
  *Example*: Jag har slutat med kaffe (*I have quit coffee*).
- **sluta på**: To end at or finish.  
  *Example*: Filmen slutar på en cliffhanger (*The movie ends on a cliffhanger*).

---

## BEDÖMA (to judge)
**Base Verb**: bedöma — Refers to evaluating or judging something.

**Prepositional Combinations**:
- **bedöma som**: To judge as.  
  *Example*: Han bedömdes som skyldig (*He was judged as guilty*).
- **bedöma efter**: To judge based on.  
  *Example*: Vi bedömer efter resultatet (*We judge based on the result*).

---

## TÄNKA (to think)
**Base Verb**: tänka — Refers to the act of thinking.

**Prepositional Combinations**:
- **tänka på**: To think about.  
  *Example*: Jag tänker på dig (*I am thinking about you*).
- **tänka över**: To consider or reflect on.  
  *Example*: Jag måste tänka över mitt beslut (*I need to think over my decision*).

---

## FÖRSTÅ (to understand)
**Base Verb**: förstå — Refers to understanding something.

**Prepositional Combinations**:
- **förstå av**: To understand from.  
  *Example*: Jag förstod av hans ton att han var arg (*I understood from his tone that he was angry*).
- **förstå för**: To understand for someone.  
  *Example*: Kan du förstå det här för mig? (*Can you understand this for me?*).

---

## KÄNNA (to feel)
**Base Verb**: känna — Refers to feeling or knowing.

**Prepositional Combinations**:
- **känna igen**: To recognize.  
  *Example*: Jag kände igen honom direkt (*I recognized him immediately*).
- **känna av**: To sense or feel.  
  *Example*: Jag känner av en förkylning (*I feel a cold coming on*).
- **känna för**: To feel like or have an inclination.  
  *Example*: Jag känner för att gå ut (*I feel like going out*).
