# Servering – service / serving vocabulary reference

## 1) Core vocabulary
- servering – service
- servera – to serve
- kund – customer
- restaurang – restaurant
- bartender – bartender
- kock – cook
- meny – menu
- beställning – order
- betalning – payment
- notan – bill
- vänta – to wait
- leverera – to deliver
- ta emot – to receive

### Useful phrases
- Jag vill beställa mat. – I want to order food.
- Servitören kommer snart. – The waiter will come soon.
- Kan vi få menyn? – Can we have the menu?
- Vi betalar räkningen nu. – We are paying the bill now.

### Example sentences
- Serveringen var snabb och trevlig. – The service was quick and pleasant.
- Kocken lagade maten i köket. – The cook prepared the food in the kitchen.
- Kunden väntade på sin beställning. – The customer waited for the order.

### Quick summary
- servering = service
- servera = serve
- meny = menu
- beställning = order
- betalning = payment
- notan = bill
- kund = customer

This page is useful in restaurants, cafés, and dining situations.
