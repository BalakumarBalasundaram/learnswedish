# Restaurang – restaurant vocabulary reference

## 1) Core vocabulary
- restaurang – restaurant
- café – café
- bord – table
- stol – chair
- meny – menu
- kund – customer
- servitör – waiter
- servitris – waitress
- kock – cook
- kök – kitchen
- betalning – payment
- lunch – lunch
- middag – dinner

### Useful phrases
- Vi går till restaurangen. – We go to the restaurant.
- Har du ett bord för två? – Do you have a table for two?
- Kan vi få menyn, tack? – Can we have the menu, please?
- Jag vill boka ett bord. – I want to book a table.

### Example sentences
- Restaurangen är folktom och lugn. – The restaurant is quiet and empty.
- Servitören tog vår beställning. – The waiter took our order.
- Vi betalade notan i slutet. – We paid the bill at the end.

### Quick summary
- restaurang = restaurant
- bord = table
- meny = menu
- servitör = waiter
- kock = cook
- betalning = payment

This page is practical for restaurant conversations and everyday dining.
