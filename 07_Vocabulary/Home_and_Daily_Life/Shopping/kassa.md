# Kassa – checkout vocabulary reference

## 1) Core vocabulary
- kassa – checkout / cash register
- betalning – payment
- kort – card
- kontanter – cash
- kvitto – receipt
- kund – customer
- säljare – salesperson
- bag – bag
- varukorg – basket
- skanna – to scan
- betala – to pay
- ta emot – to receive

### Useful phrases
- Jag går till kassan. – I go to the checkout.
- Jag vill betala med kort. – I want to pay by card.
- Kan jag få kvittot? – Can I have the receipt?
- Det här är min shoppingkorg. – This is my shopping basket.

### Example sentences
- Kassan är lång, men jag väntar. – The checkout is long, but I am waiting.
- Säljaren skannar varorna. – The salesperson scans the items.
- Jag betalade med kontanter. – I paid with cash.

### Quick summary
- kassa = checkout
- betalning = payment
- kort = card
- kontanter = cash
- kvitto = receipt
- betala = pay
