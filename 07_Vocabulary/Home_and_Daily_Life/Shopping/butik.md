# Butik – shop vocabulary reference

## 1) Core vocabulary
- butik – shop
- affär – store
- kund – customer
- säljare – salesperson
- kassa – checkout
- varor – goods
- prislapp – price tag
- erbjudande – offer / sale
- kvitto – receipt
- betalning – payment
- handla – to shop
- köpa – to buy
- sälja – to sell

### Useful phrases
- Jag går till butiken. – I go to the shop.
- Jag vill handla mat. – I want to shop for food.
- Var finns kassorna? – Where are the checkouts?
- Kan jag få kvittot? – Can I have the receipt?

### Example sentences
- Den här butiken har många varor. – This shop has many goods.
- Säljaren hjälper mig. – The salesperson helps me.
- Jag köpte en tröja och betalade i kassan. – I bought a sweater and paid at the checkout.

### Quick summary
- butik = shop
- affär = store
- kund = customer
- säljare = salesperson
- kassa = checkout
- köpa = buy
- handla = shop

This is a useful everyday shopping word for stores and errands.
