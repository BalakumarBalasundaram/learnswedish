# Cinderella – vocabulary reference

## 1) Main characters and story words

### Characters
- Cinderella – Askungen
- styvmor – stepmother
- styvsystrar – stepsisters
- prins – prince
- troll – fairy
- häst – horse
- vagn – carriage

### Story objects
- sko – shoe
- ball – ball
- klänning – dress
- glasögon? no – not applicable
- klocka – clock
- dröm – dream

### Action words
- arbeta – to work
- dansa – to dance
- förlora – to lose
- hitta – to find
- gå – to go
- springa – to run
- glömma – to forget
- förvandla – to transform

### Useful phrases
- Askungen arbetade hårt hemma. – Cinderella worked hard at home.
- Hon ville gå till balen. – She wanted to go to the ball.
- En fe förvandlade hennes klänning. – A fairy transformed her dress.
- Prinsen dansade med Askungen. – The prince danced with Cinderella.
- Hon tappade skorna. – She lost her shoe.
- Prinsen hittade skorna. – The prince found the shoe.

---

## 2) Example sentences
- Askungen var trött men glad. – Cinderella was tired but happy.
- Hon gick till balen i en vacker klänning. – She went to the ball in a beautiful dress.
- Prinsen ville hitta den rätta flickan. – The prince wanted to find the right girl.
- Hon sprang hem innan klockan slog tolv. – She ran home before the clock struck twelve.

## 4) Story summary
Askungen arbetade hårt hemma för sin styvmor och styvsystrar. En fe förvandlade hennes enkla kläder till en vacker klänning och gav henne en vagn. Hon gick till balen och dansade med prinsen. Hon förlorade skorna, och prinsen hittade den. Till slut blev hon fri och lycklig.

English: Cinderella worked hard at home for her stepmother and stepsisters. A fairy transformed her simple clothes into a beautiful dress and gave her a carriage. She went to the ball and danced with the prince. She lost her shoe, and the prince found it. In the end she became free and happy.
