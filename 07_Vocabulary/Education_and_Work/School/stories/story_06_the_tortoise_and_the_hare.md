# The Tortoise and the Hare – vocabulary reference

## 1) Main characters and story words

### Characters
- sköldpadda – tortoise
- hare – hare
- löpare – runner
- vän – friend

### Story objects
- bana – track
- mål – finish line
- skog – forest
- väg – road

### Action words
- springa – to run
- sova – to sleep
- vänta – to wait
- vinna – to win
- förlora – to lose
- gå – to walk

### Useful phrases
- Sköldpaddan och haren tävlade. – The tortoise and the hare raced.
- Haren sprang snabbt. – The hare ran quickly.
- Sköldpaddan gick långsamt men stadigt. – The tortoise walked slowly but steadily.
- Haren somnade. – The hare slept.
- Sköldpaddan vann. – The tortoise won.

---

## 2) Example sentences
- Haren trodde att han skulle vinna. – The hare thought he would win.
- Sköldpaddan var stadig och tålamodig. – The tortoise was steady and patient.
- Långsam, men säker, segrade sköldpaddan. – Slow but sure, the tortoise won.

---

## 3) Quick vocabulary list
- sköldpadda = tortoise
- hare = hare
- springa = run
- sova = sleep
- vinna = win
- förlora = lose
- långsam = slow
- snabb = fast

---

## 4) Story summary
Sköldpaddan och haren tävlade i en lopp. Haren sprang snabbt men somnade under vägen. Sköldpaddan fortsatte långsamt men bestämt och vann loppet. Detta visar att tålamod ofta leder till framgång.

English: The tortoise and the hare raced. The hare ran quickly but fell asleep along the way. The tortoise continued slowly but steadily and won the race. This shows that patience often leads to success.
