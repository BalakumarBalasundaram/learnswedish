# The Emperor’s New Clothes – vocabulary reference

## 1) Main characters and story words

### Characters
- kejsaren – emperor
- två skräddare – two tailors
- barn – child

### Story objects
- kläder – clothes
- tyg – fabric
- tråd – thread
- kungligt palats – royal palace

### Action words
- sy – to sew
- säga – to say
- se – to see
- skratta – to laugh
- berätta – to tell
- förstå – to understand

### Useful phrases
- Kejsaren ville ha nya kläder. – The emperor wanted new clothes.
- Två skräddare sa att tyget var magiskt. – Two tailors said the fabric was magical.
- Ingen ville säga sanningen. – Nobody wanted to tell the truth.
- Ett barn sa att kejsaren var naken. – A child said the emperor was naked.

---

## 2) Example sentences
- Kejsaren trodde att han var klädd i magiska kläder. – The emperor thought he was dressed in magical clothes.
- Alla i hovet ville inte säga sanningen. – Everyone at court did not want to tell the truth.
- Barnet skrattade och sa sanningen. – The child laughed and told the truth.

---

## 3) Quick vocabulary list
- kejsare = emperor
- kläder = clothes
- tyg = fabric
- sy = sew
- säga = say
- barn = child
- sanningen = truth
- skratta = laugh

---

## 4) Story summary
Kejsaren blev lurad av två skräddare som sa att de sydde magiska kläder som bara kloka människor kunde se. Ingen ville säga sanningen, men ett barn sa att kejsaren var naken. Det visade att sanningen ofta kommer fram till slut.

English: The emperor was deceived by two tailors who said they were sewing magical clothes that only wise people could see. Nobody wanted to tell the truth, but a child said that the emperor was naked. This showed that the truth often comes out in the end.
