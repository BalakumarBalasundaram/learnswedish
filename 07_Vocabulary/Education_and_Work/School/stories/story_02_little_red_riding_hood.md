# Little Red Riding Hood – vocabulary reference

## 1) Main characters and story words

### Characters
- Little Red Riding Hood – Röda Rödluvan
- varg – wolf
- mormor – grandmother
- skog – forest
- jägare – hunter

### Story objects
- korg – basket
- klänning – dress
- kapell – hood / hooded cloak
- väg – path
- hus – house
- träd – trees

### Action words
- gå – to walk
- bära – to carry
- träffa – to meet
- prata – to talk
- springa – to run
- hjälpa – to help
- skära – to cut
- rädda – to save

### Useful phrases
- Röda Rödluvan gick till sin mormor. – Little Red Riding Hood went to her grandmother.
- Hon bar en korg med mat. – She carried a basket with food.
- Hon mötte en varg i skogen. – She met a wolf in the forest.
- Vargen ville hjälpa henne. – The wolf wanted to help her.
- Vargen sprang till mormors hus. – The wolf ran to grandmother’s house.
- Jägaren räddade mormor och flickan. – The hunter saved the grandmother and the girl.

---

## 2) Example sentences
- Röda Rödluvan gick genom skogen. – Little Red Riding Hood walked through the forest.
- Hon bar en korg med frukt. – She carried a basket with fruit.
- Vargen pratade med henne. – The wolf talked with her.
- Jägaren kom och räddade dem. – The hunter came and saved them.

## 4) Story summary
Röda Rödluvan gick till sin mormor med en korg med mat. I skogen träffade hon en varg som ville lura henne. Vargen sprang till mormors hus och gömde sig i sängen. Jägaren kom och räddade dem. Röda Rödluvan lärde sig att vara försiktig.

English: Little Red Riding Hood went to her grandmother with a basket of food. In the forest she met a wolf who wanted to trick her. The wolf ran to grandmother’s house and hid in the bed. The hunter came and saved them. Little Red Riding Hood learned to be careful.
