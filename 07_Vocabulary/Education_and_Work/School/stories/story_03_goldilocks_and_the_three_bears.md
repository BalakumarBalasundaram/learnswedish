# Goldilocks and the Three Bears – vocabulary reference

## 1) Main characters and story words

### Characters
- Goldilocks – Goldlocks
- de tre björnarna – the three bears
- björn – bear
- mamma björn – mother bear
- pappa björn – father bear
- lilla björn – little bear

### Story objects
- stol – chair
- säng – bed
- skål – bowl
- gröt – porridge
- hus – house
- trädgård – garden

### Action words
- smaka – to taste
- sitta – to sit
- ligga – to lie
- hitta – to find
- springa – to run
- vakna – to wake up
- skälla – to bark

### Useful phrases
- Goldlocks kom in i huset. – Goldilocks came into the house.
- Hon smakade på gröten. – She tasted the porridge.
- Hon satt i stolarna. – She sat in the chairs.
- Hon la sig i sängen. – She lay down in the bed.
- Björnarna kom hem. – The bears came home.
- Hon sprang iväg. – She ran away.

---

## 2) Example sentences
- Goldlocks hittade ett hus i skogen. – Goldilocks found a house in the forest.
- Hon tyckte att gröten var för varm. – She thought the porridge was too hot.
- Den lilla stolen var perfekt. – The little chair was perfect.
- Björnarna blev arga. – The bears became angry.

## 4) Story summary
Goldlocks gick in i huset i skogen och hittade gröt, stolar och en säng. Hon smakade på gröten, satte sig i stolarna och lade sig i sängen. Plötsligt kom de tre björnarna hem och blev arga. Goldlocks sprang iväg i panik.

English: Goldilocks walked into the house in the forest and found porridge, chairs, and a bed. She tasted the porridge, sat in the chairs, and lay in the bed. Suddenly the three bears came home and became angry. Goldilocks ran away in panic.
