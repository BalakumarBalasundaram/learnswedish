# Twinkle, Twinkle, Little Star – vocabulary reference

## 1) Key words
- stjärna – star
- liten – little
- himmel – sky
- glänsa – to shine
- sitta – to sit
- universum – universe
- ljus – light
- natt – night

### Useful phrases
- Stjärna, stjärna, lilla stjärna. – Star, star, little star.
- Hur underbart du lyser. – How wonderfully you shine.
- Du lyser i den mörka natten. – You shine in the dark night.
- Jag ser dig i himlen. – I see you in the sky.

---

## 2) Example sentences
- Jag ser en stjärna i himlen. – I see a star in the sky.
- Den lilla stjärnan lyser starkt. – The little star shines brightly.
- Vi tittar på himlen på natten. – We look at the sky at night.

---

## 3) Quick vocabulary list
- stjärna = star

- himmel = sky

## 4) Song summary
The song is about a little star shining in the night sky. It is a simple and gentle song often used to teach children basic words.
