# Row, Row, Row Your Boat – vocabulary reference

## 1) Key words
- ro – to row
- båt – boat
- vatten – water
- glada – happy
- låt – song
- flod – river
- simma – to swim
- fortsätta – to continue

### Useful phrases
- Vi roddar båten över vattnet. – We row the boat across the water.
- Båten glider på floden. – The boat glides on the river.
- Vi fortsätter att sjunga. – We continue singing.

---

## 2) Example sentences
- Båten ligger på vattnet. – The boat is on the water.
- Vi roddar tillsammans. – We row together.
- Barnen sjunger medan de roddar. – The children sing while they row.

---

## 3) Quick vocabulary list
- ro = row
- båt = boat
- vatten = water
- flod = river
- glada = happy
- låt = song

---

## 4) Song summary
This song is about rowing a boat and singing together. It is useful for learning verbs related to movement and water.
