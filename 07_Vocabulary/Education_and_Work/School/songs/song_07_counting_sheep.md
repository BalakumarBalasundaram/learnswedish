# Counting Sheep – vocabulary reference

## 1) Key words
- får – sheep
- räkna – to count
- sova – to sleep
- natt – night
- dröm – dream
- tyst – quiet
- himmel – sky
- lullaby – vaggvisa

### Useful phrases
- Vi räknar får i den tysta natten. – We count sheep in the quiet night.
- Barnet vill sova. – The child wants to sleep.
- Lullaby hjälper till att slappna av. – A lullaby helps to relax.

---

## 2) Example sentences
- Jag räknar får innan jag somnar. – I count sheep before I fall asleep.
- Natten är tyst och lugn. – The night is quiet and calm.
- Jag drömmer om himlen. – I dream about the sky.

---

This is a calm lullaby about sheep and sleep. It is good for learning night-time vocabulary and relaxation words.
