# Old MacDonald Had a Farm – vocabulary reference

## 1) Key words
- gård – farm
- ko – cow
- gris – pig
- hund – dog
- katt – cat
- fågel – bird
- mjölk – milk
- ljud – sound

### Useful phrases
- På gården hade MacDonald en ko. – On the farm, MacDonald had a cow.
- Varje djur gör sitt ljud. – Every animal makes its sound.
- Jag hör ett ljud i hagen. – I hear a sound in the field.
- Gården är full av liv. – The farm is full of life.

---

## 2) Example sentences
- På gården bor många djur. – Many animals live on the farm.
- Kossan ger mjölk. – The cow gives milk.
- Hunden skäller högt. – The dog barks loudly.

## 4) Song summary
This song is about a farmer and the different animals on his farm. It uses repeated sounds and is helpful for learning animals and simple verbs.
