# Inversion i frågor

> 🧠 **Mental model — the swap dance:** a yes/no question is a huvudsats where subject and verb trade seats: "Du är glad." → "Är du glad?". Same seat-2 rule as always — the verb keeps seat 2, the subject just moves to seat 1's old neighbor.

## 📜 Background — why English needs "do" but Swedish doesn't

Question inversion is really just the V2 rule (see [01_huvudsats.md](01_huvudsats.md)) with an empty seat 1: the finite verb simply moves to the very front. English used to work exactly the same way (*"Know you the way?"*), but as English lost general V2 order, this kind of full-verb inversion became impossible for ordinary verbs — so English had to invent a workaround called **do-support**, inserting a meaningless helper verb ("**Do** you know the way?") just so *something* could sit in front of the subject. Swedish never needed that patch: since every finite verb can still freely invert, **any** verb — not just auxiliaries — can front for a question (Bor hon här? / Do**es** she live here?).

## Examples

- Du är glad. → **Är** du glad? (Are you happy?)
- Hon bor här. → **Bor** hon här? (Does she live here?)
- Ni har ätit. → **Har** ni ätit? (Have you eaten?)
