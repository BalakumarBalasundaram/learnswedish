# Subjekt/objekt: grundstruktur

> 🧠 **Mental model — the conveyor belt:** Swedish word order is a strict SVO conveyor belt — Subjekt → Verb → Objekt — and nothing is allowed to jump the belt except the "seat 2" verb rule from huvudsats.

## 📜 Background — SVO is a relatively recent arrangement

Most historical linguists believe Proto-Indo-European (the ancestor of Swedish, English, Hindi, Russian, and hundreds of other languages) was actually **SOV** (subject-object-verb) — the verb came last, not in the middle. Over thousands of years, the Germanic branch (among others) drifted toward SVO, likely because a fixed subject-verb-object order became a reliable way to signal grammatical roles *without* relying on case endings, right as those case endings were eroding away (see the object-form pronoun fossils in [02_pronomen_objektsformer.md](../01_Ordklasser/02_pronomen_objektsformer.md)). So the strict SVO conveyor belt you're learning today is essentially a structural replacement for a case system Swedish mostly lost.

## Examples

- **Läraren** (S) **läser** (V) **boken** (O). (The teacher reads the book.)
- **Hunden** (S) **jagar** (V) **katten** (O). (The dog chases the cat.)
- **Vi** (S) **äter** (V) **middag** (O). (We eat dinner.)
