# Satsbyggnad: ordföljd och fokus

> 🧠 **Mental model — Swedish puts the spotlight in one place:** the first position in the clause is for the part you want to highlight, but the verb still has to stay in the right place. The sentence is not only about grammar; it is also about focus.

## The basic idea

Swedish often lets you move one element to the front for emphasis, but the verb still has to follow the main clause rule. That means the writer must think about both meaning and structure at the same time.

## Questions to ask

- What do I want to put in focus?
- Is this a main clause or a subordinate clause?
- Did I keep the finite verb in the right place?
- Did I move the right element to the front?

## Pattern reminders

- In a main clause, the finite verb stays in second position.
- In a subordinate clause, the word order changes.
- Fronted time, place, or object phrases change the sentence rhythm, but not the core meaning.

## Examples

- **I går** såg jag filmen.
- **På caféet** satt vi länge.
- **Om jag hinner** kommer jag senare.
- **Eftersom det regnade** stannade vi hemma.
