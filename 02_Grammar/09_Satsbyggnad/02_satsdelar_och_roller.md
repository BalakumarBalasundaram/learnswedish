# Satsbyggnad: satsdelar och roller

> 🧠 **Mental model — assign each word a job:** nouns name the participants, verbs carry the action, adjectives describe, adverbs modify, and prepositions connect. A clear sentence depends on each part doing one job only.

## Main roles in a sentence

| Role | What it does | Common forms |
|---|---|---|
| Subject | Tells who or what does or is something | pronoun, noun phrase |
| Verb | Carries the action or state | finite verb, auxiliary + main verb |
| Object | Receives the action | direct object, indirect object |
| Adverbial | Adds time, place, manner, reason, or direction | adverb, prepositional phrase |
| Attribute | Describes a noun | adjective, determiner, numeral |
| Connector | Links one clause to another | conjunction, subjunktion, relative pronoun |

## Why this matters

If you know the role, you can choose the right form. For example:

- a pronoun may need object form after a verb or preposition
- an adjective must agree with the noun it describes
- a preposition must show the relation you want, not just the translation you remember

## Quick examples

- **Hon** såg **mig** i går.  
  - hon = subject
  - såg = verb
  - mig = object
  - i går = adverbial

- **Den stora hunden** sprang **mot huset**.  
  - den stora hunden = subject phrase
  - sprang = verb
  - mot huset = direction phrase

- **Jag tror att han kommer i kväll.**  
  - jag tror = main clause
  - att han kommer i kväll = subordinate clause
