# Prepositioner: relation och tillhörighet

> 🧠 **Mental model — the belonging line:** **hos** places you at a person or place, **till** points to a recipient or destination, and **av** can mark origin or possession-like relations. Ask: where am I, where is it going, or where did it come from?

## 📜 Background — the same map used for possession and destination

A lot of Swedish preposition usage comes from the same underlying mental map: movement, location, and relation are all connected. **Till** is not only physical direction; it also marks the receiver of an action: *boken till Anna*. **Av** can mark the source or creator: *ett hus av trä* (made of wood), *en present av min vän* (a gift from my friend). **Hos** anchors the meaning at a person or provider: *hos doktorn*, *hos mig*. These patterns are very old and very stable because languages repeatedly recycle concrete space relations to build abstract social and possessive meanings.

## Examples

- **till** Anna (to Anna), **till** skolan (to school)
- **av** trä (made of wood), **av** min vän (from my friend)
- **hos** doktorn (at the doctor's), **hos** mig (at my place)
