# Prepositioner: jämförelse och omfattning

> 🧠 **Mental model — measuring against a line:** **än** sets the comparison target, **per** spreads something across units, and **bland** places something inside a group. Ask: compared to what, per what unit, or among which group?

## 📜 Background — prepositions as measurement tools

Swedish uses prepositions not only for space and time, but also for how we measure relationships. **Än** is the comparison pivot in phrases like *större än*, while **per** turns one total into a rate: *50 kronor per person*. **Bland** positions something inside a set, so the meaning is not location in a room but membership in a group. English uses the same idea with **than**, **per**, and **among**.

## Examples

- större **än** jag (bigger than me)
- snabbare **än** igår (faster than yesterday)
- 50 kronor **per** person (50 kronor per person)
- populär **bland** ungdomar (popular among young people)
