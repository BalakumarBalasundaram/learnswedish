# Prepositioner: medel och sätt

> 🧠 **Mental model — the tool in your hand:** **med** names the companion or instrument you use, **utan** removes it, and **genom** points to the channel or path something passes through. Ask: with what, without what, or through what?

## 📜 Background — from company and physical passage to abstract method

These prepositions are easy to misuse because they sit right on the border between concrete and abstract meaning. **Med** originally means being together with something or someone, but it also became the normal way to mark an instrument: *skriva med penna* (write with a pen). **Genom** is literally a path-word — moving through a space — and then extended to means or method: *genom att öva* (by practicing), *genom e-post* (via email). The shift is the same in English: we borrow physical passage words like **through**, **with**, and **by** to explain abstract method.

## Examples

- **med** buss (by bus), **med** en penna (with a pen)
- **utan** kaffe (without coffee), **utan** hjälp (without help)
- **genom** dörren (through the door), **genom** att öva (by practicing)
- **via** mejl (via email)
