# Prepositioner: riktning

> 🧠 **Mental model — three compass arrows:** "till" = an arrow landing at a destination (till skolan), "mot" = an arrow moving toward without necessarily arriving (mot centrum), "från" = an arrow leaving its origin (från jobbet). The direction the arrow points tells you which preposition to use.

## 📜 Background — from physical motion to abstract possession

**till** and **från** are directly cognate with English **till/until** and **from** — both pairs trace back to the same Proto-Germanic directional roots. But **till** shows an especially interesting extra step: the same word that marks physical motion toward a destination (till skolan, "to school") has also been recruited to mark **possession/recipient** (boken till Anna, "the book for/to Anna"). This motion-to-possession extension is a well-documented metaphor pattern — English does the identical thing with "to" ("give it **to** her") — treating the abstract idea of something belonging to or being destined for someone as if it were physically moving toward them.

## Examples

- **till** skolan (to school) — Han sprang **till** stationen. (He ran to the station.)
- **mot** centrum (toward downtown) — Bilen körde **mot** oss. (The car drove toward us.)
- **från** jobbet (from work) — Hon kom **från** Spanien. (She came from Spain.)
