# Pronomen: objektsformer

usage and purpose of object forms of pronouns (me, you, him, her, it, us, them) in Swedish.

## Object forms of personal pronouns
| Subject form | Object form |
|--------------|-------------|
| jag          | mig         |
| du           | dig         |
| han          | honom       |
| hon          | henne       |
| den          | den         |
| det          | det         |
| vi           | oss         |
| ni           | er          |
| de           | dem         |

## Usage rules
- The object form is used when the pronoun is the direct or indirect object of a verb.
- It is also used after prepositions.
- In some cases, the object form can be used for emphasis or contrast.

## Examples
1. Jag såg **honom** igår. (I saw him yesterday.)
2. Hon gav **mig** en bok. (She gave me a book.)
3. Vi pratade med **dem**. (We talked to them.)
4. Kan du hjälpa **oss**? (Can you help us?)
5. De gick till affären utan **er**. (They went to the store without you.)
6. Jag vill inte gå med **dig**. (I don't want to go with you.)
7. Hon älskar **honom**. (She loves him.)
8. Vi såg **den** i parken. (We saw it in the park.)
9. De gav **det** till mig. (They gave it to me.)
10. Jag pratade med **honom** om det. (I talked to him about it.)
11. Hon berättade för **oss** en hemlighet. (She told us a secret.)
12. De frågade **er** om hjälp. (They asked you for help.)
13. Jag såg **dem** på bio. (I saw them at the movies.)
14. Hon gav **mig** en present. (She gave me a present.)
15. Vi pratade med **honom** om resan. (We talked to him about the trip.)
