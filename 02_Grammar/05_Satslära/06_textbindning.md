# Textbindning: först, sedan, därför

> 🧠 **Mental model — signposts on the road:** först/sedan are timeline signposts, därför is a cause-effect signpost, dessutom is a "one more thing" signpost. Unlike konjunktioner/subjunktioner, these connect whole *sentences* along the story's road, not clauses within one sentence.

## 📜 Background — built from "there" + a preposition

**Därför** is a transparent compound: **där** (there) + **för** (for) — literally "there-for", i.e. "for that [reason]". Swedish has a whole productive family of these **där-** compounds (därför, därmed, däremot, därifrån), each built the same way: an old "there" pointing word fused with a preposition. English used to have the identical productive pattern — **therefore, thereby, therein, wherein** — but it's now fossilized and mostly limited to formal/legal writing, while Swedish kept the pattern fully alive and everyday. Discourse connectors like these are also typically among the **last** things language learners master, because they operate above the sentence level, linking whole chunks of a story together rather than words within one clause.

## Examples

- **Först** åt vi frukost, **sedan** gick vi till skolan. (First we ate breakfast, then we went to school.)
- Det regnade, **därför** stannade vi hemma. (It was raining, therefore we stayed home.)
- Hon var trött. **Dessutom** hade hon huvudvärk. (She was tired. Moreover, she had a headache.)
