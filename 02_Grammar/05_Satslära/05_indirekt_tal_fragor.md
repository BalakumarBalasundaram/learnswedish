# Indirekt tal (frågor)

> 🧠 **Mental model — the question loses its mark:** "Var bor du?" becomes "Hon frågade **var** jag bodde" — the question mark and inversion disappear, the frågeord (or "om" for yes/no questions) turns into a subjunktion, and bisats word order (BIFF) takes over.

## 📜 Background — a spatial word repurposed as a question-glue

**Om** originally meant "around/about" as a purely spatial/topic preposition ("en bok **om** Sverige", a book about Sweden) — the same grammaticalization path as many words in [04_Prepositioner](../04_Prepositioner/01_tid.md). For yes/no indirect questions, Swedish recruited that same "about" word to mean "whether", following the logic "she asked **about** [whether] I was coming" — English does the identical thing with **if**, which originally had nothing to do with questions either. It's a common pattern: languages rarely invent a brand-new word for an abstract grammatical job; they repurpose an existing everyday word whose meaning is loosely compatible.

## Examples

- Direkt: "Var bor du?" → Indirekt: Hon frågade **var** jag bodde. (She asked where I lived.)
- Direkt: "Kommer du imorgon?" → Indirekt: Han undrade **om** jag skulle komma imorgon. (He wondered if I would come tomorrow.)
