# Relativa pronomen: som, vilket, vars

> 🧠 **Mental model — three hooks reaching back:** som = general hook grabbing a noun (who/that/which), vilket = hook grabbing a whole previous clause/idea, vars = possessive hook grabbing an owner (whose). Pick the hook by what you're reaching for: a thing, an idea, or an owner.

## 📜 Background — how "as" became "who/which/that"

**Som** did not start out as a relative pronoun at all — it comes from an old Scandinavian comparison particle meaning roughly "as/like" (still visible in **såsom**, "such as"). Over time, Swedish (and Norwegian, Danish) generalized this single comparison word into the *one* all-purpose relative pronoun, covering the job that English splits across **who/which/that**. This is why **som** never changes for person, gender, or number — it never fully became a true pronoun grammatically, it's still behaving like the old invariant comparison particle it descended from. **Vilket** and **vars**, by contrast, kept the older Germanic wh-forms directly cognate with English **which** and **whose**.

## Examples

- Boken **som** jag läser är spännande. (The book that I'm reading is exciting.)
- Huset, **vilket** förvånade oss alla, var till salu. (The house, which surprised us all, was for sale.)
- Mannen **vars** bil är trasig behöver hjälp. (The man whose car is broken needs help.)
