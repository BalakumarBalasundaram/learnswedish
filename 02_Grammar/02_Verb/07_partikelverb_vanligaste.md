# Partikelverb: vanligaste

> 🧠 **Mental model — the particle compass:** one base verb, four directions on a compass, four meanings. gå in / gå ut / gå upp / gå ner (go in/out/up/down) — memorize the compass around each common verb rather than each combination separately.

## 📜 Background — why spatial words are the oldest building blocks

Direction words like **in, ut, upp, ner** are among the most ancient, stable vocabulary in any language — spatial orientation (where is it relative to me?) was one of the first things human language needed to express, long before abstract vocabulary existed. That's why almost every verb in every Germanic language can combine with the same small set of directional particles: the particles are a closed, primal set, while the verbs they attach to are the open, ever-growing part. Learning the "compass" around one verb (gå in/ut/upp/ner) trains a pattern that transfers directly to dozens of other verbs (komma in/ut/upp/ner, släppa in/ut/upp/ner) — the same four spatial primitives, recombined.

## Examples

- **gå in / gå ut / gå upp / gå ner** — go in / out / up / down
- **ta bort** — Ta **bort** skräpet. (Remove the trash.)
- **sätta på** — Sätt **på** tv:n. (Turn on the TV.)
- **stänga av** — Stäng **av** lampan. (Turn off the light.)
- **komma ihåg** — Kom **ihåg** att ringa mig. (Remember to call me.)
